100% Local & Private Storage

Zero Cloud Uploads & Zero Tracking

Your spreadsheets and monthly figures are parsed strictly inside your browser's memory and stored only in this device's localStorage. Nothing is ever sent to any external server.

Why your data has minimal exposure even if inspected locally:
  • No Account Numbers, Sort Codes, or Logins: Only shorthand labels (like MC or HL isa shares) and balances are saved.
  • Read-Only Numbers: No bank tokens or payment credentials exist anywhere in this file.
  • Best Practice: Use a device login password/PIN and avoid using this app on shared public computers.
